Macrofinancial research group

Exploring the connections between economies and markets.

Philomath Quantitative Research (PQR) is a macrofinancial research group interested in how macroeconomic dynamics and financial markets interact. Quantitative reasoning, economic models and evidence offer complementary ways to investigate those connections.

This website is taking shape. Information about the group, its research interests and future projects will be added here as it becomes available.

An introduction

A quantitative perspective on the macrofinancial world.

PQR brings together a broad interest in macroeconomics, finance and the analytical methods used to study them. The aim of this site is to provide a clear home for the group's identity and, as material becomes ready for publication, its work.
