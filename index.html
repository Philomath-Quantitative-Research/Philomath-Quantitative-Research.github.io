Macroeconomic research for forecasting and systematic trading.

Philomath Quantitative Research (PQR) focuses on macroeconomic and macrofinancial analysis to identify meaningful signals, develop and evaluate quantitative models, produce forecasts and investigate systematic trading strategies.

Our research agenda connects economic theory, financial data and empirical testing. The website is under development; research outputs and project details will be added as they become available.

An introduction

From economic information to testable market strategies.

The goal is to study how macroeconomic developments and financial conditions relate to market behaviour, identify predictive information, and build models that can be assessed through transparent empirical tests. Forecasting and systematic trading are potential applications of that research.
